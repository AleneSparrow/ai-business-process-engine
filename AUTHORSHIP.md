# Authorship

**Alena Kulish** invented Flywheel (this repository: AI Business Process Engine), specified how it should work, and directed its implementation. She is the sole founder and the sole copyright holder.

She is not claiming that every line was typed by hand. She is claiming — and this repository is the public record — that a person who knows the business problem from the inside can design the process, manage the work, and use neural networks as the workforce that drafts the software.

## What is human-authored

- The problem: businesses lose inbound leads because people are slow, busy, or inconsistent. That knowledge comes from operating those processes, not from a model.
- The product idea: a full lead-to-sale engine that any business can configure, without custom build-out per company.
- The architecture: a deterministic process engine and decision router; AI may extract meaning and rephrase approved wording, and must not make the business decision.
- The specifications, reviews, accept/reject decisions, and product sequencing.

Under U.S. Copyright Office guidance on works containing AI-generated material, the human who conceives the work, directs the tools, and selects what is kept is the author. That human is Alena Kulish.
