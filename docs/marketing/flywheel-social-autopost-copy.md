## Brand posts (LinkedIn / X / Instagram caption)

**1 — category gap**

Most tools take a name and a number and hand you a message to return. Flywheel carries the inquiry the rest of the way: qualifying, the price question, follow-up, and a booked job. Any business. No per-company build. 7-day trial, $199/mo after.

**2 — cycle**

Capture is the easy half. Flywheel answers, qualifies, follows up with people who go quiet, and books the job — then hands you a prepared piece of work, not an inbox. Live in about 20 minutes.

**3 — not lead gen**

Flywheel does not find you new customers. It works the inquiries you already get, all the way to a booked job or an approved quote. Collecting the money stays with you.

**4 — safety, not the offer**

The reason to use Flywheel is that the conversation finishes. The reason it is safe: the AI only rewords content you already approved. It identifies itself as AI in every conversation.

**5 — zero-config**

Nothing in the engine is written for one industry. You describe the business; it adapts. Law practices are the first door we opened — not what the product is.
