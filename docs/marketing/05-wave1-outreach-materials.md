### Hero

**H1:** Every inquiry answered. Every consult on the calendar.

**Subhead:** Flywheel carries a person who already contacted your firm from first message to a booked meeting — built so the AI can only follow the script you approve. Not a policy. An architecture.

**CTA:** Start your 7\-day free trial — $199/mo, no setup call.

### The problem

Every missed call is a lead you already paid for. The average cost per lead in legal services runs close to $649 — and industry data shows most callers who hit voicemail never call back. A full\-time intake specialist costs $55,000–$80,000 a year fully loaded, and even a great one can't answer at 11pm on a Sunday.

### How Flywheel is different

Most AI intake tools are a chatbot wrapped around a general\-purpose language model, steered by a prompt. Prompts can be argued with, jailbroken, or simply drift over a long conversation — and for a law firm, that's not a UX risk, it's a bar\-complaint risk.

Flywheel works differently. The AI never decides what to tell your client — it only rewrites the wording of a script you control ("Business DNA"). Case qualification, escalation rules, and what the assistant is and isn't allowed to say all live in the underlying engine, not in a prompt the AI could talk itself out of. The AI identifies itself as AI from the first message, every time — the standard California and New York already require by law.

### What it does

- Answers inbound leads on your website, day or night
- Qualifies the case against the criteria you set — practice area, urgency, location
- Books a consultation in Flywheel so you receive a prepared matter, not an empty inbox
- Follows up if they go quiet after the first reply
- Escalates to you (or your staff) exactly when your rules say to
- Never estimates case outcomes, gives legal advice, or promises a result — by design, not by request

### Built for solo practices

Flywheel Starter is built for exactly one attorney, one jurisdiction — set up in about 20 minutes, no developer or IT help needed.

### FAQ

**Is this actually compliant with my state's bar rules?**
Flywheel is built to support the disclosure requirements already in effect in states like California and New York — the AI identifies itself clearly, includes a "not legal advice" notice, and always offers a path to a human. That said, bar rules vary by state and we'd always recommend a quick read of your own state's guidance before launch — we're not your compliance counsel.

**Can the AI give legal advice by accident?**
No — and that's the point. The AI only rewrites wording inside the script you approve. It has no path to invent legal analysis, estimate outcomes, or promise results, because that capability isn't built into the message pipeline at all.

**How long does setup take?**
About 20 minutes for a single\-attorney practice. No developer needed.

**What if I want more than one attorney on the account?**
Multi\-attorney support is on our roadmap — reach out and we'll let you know when it's ready for your firm.

## 5. Seven touches (founder sequence)

One connect request almost never closes. Do not invent a content factory. Do run this sequence. Each step names the next step. Full model: `07-applied-growth-concepts.md`.

1. **Saw you** — LinkedIn connect (variant A).
2. **Interested** — follow-up message in §2.
3. **Visited** — link to `/lawyers` only (not the homepage).
4. **Got value** — 90-second screencast of Flywheel selling Flywheel (live widget), or they open the corner chat.
5. **Proof** — one real audit line or a design-partner quote. No invented "38s".
6. **Offer again** — if silent, one email on day 3–5. Same job: after-hours inquiry → booked consult. No new claims.
7. **Bought** — trial or 20-minute walkthrough. Success = their widget live the same day, not a "maybe later".

If they buy on day 18, credit touch 1's channel in a 30-day window — not whichever email they opened that morning.
