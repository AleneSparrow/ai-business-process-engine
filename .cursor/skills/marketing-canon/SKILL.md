# Marketing canon (learned)

Full text: `docs/marketing/00-third-party-marketing-canon.md`. Working memory (laws, steps, pitch beats): section 11. Product USP in `CLAUDE.md` wins on claims.

## Loaded knowledge (do not re-derive from guru blogs)

**Sharp.** Brands grow by penetration of category buyers, especially light ones — not by squeezing loyalists. Double Jeopardy: small brands have fewer buyers who are slightly less loyal. Distinctiveness (recognizable assets) beats differentiation (unique meaning). Mental availability + physical availability. Repeat CEPs; do not reinvent the look every post.

**95-5 / Binet.** Most B2B targets are out of market now. Social and thought leadership build memory for later (Day 1 List). Outreach, trial, search ads convert the in-market 5%. Run both speeds. Last-click lies.

**Rumelt.** Strategy = diagnosis + guiding policy with bans + coherent actions. A revenue target is not a strategy.

**Dunford order.** Alternatives → unique attributes → value themes → who cares a lot → category last. First sentence must trigger the right assumptions. Wrong: “AI receptionist / intake.” Right: carries an existing inquiry to a booked consult or accepted quote.

**Pitch (5% only, Dunford Sales Pitch).** Setup before the product: insight → alternatives (discovery) → perfect-world criteria. Then introduction → differentiated value (2–3 themes, demo lives here) → proof → objections → the ask. Marketing legend ≠ sales pitch. Same insight in both layers.

**JTBD / Mom Test.** Hire-for-progress in circumstances. Four forces: push, pull, anxiety, habit. Interview the past: last time, what they did, what they already pay. Ban “would you buy” and “is this a good idea.”

**Moore.** Beachhead then bowling pins. Pragmatists need a whole product and a peer reference. Beachhead is kindling, not brand identity forever.

**StoryBrand grunt test.** What is it, how life improves, what to do next. Customer is the hero; product is the guide.

**Traction.** Hypothesize all 19, cheap-test ~3, scale one. Early B2B default middle ring: founder sales, a platform the ICP already lives on, thought leadership, referrals.

## Always, before shipping an artifact

1. One obstacle (Rumelt) and an explicit ban.
2. Label **brand (95%)** or **activation (5%)**. No pitch stuffed into thought leadership without a seam.
3. Dunford first-sentence test.
4. JTBD: progress + anxiety/habit, not a feature list.
5. Repeat a CEP and a distinctive asset (graphite/bronze/wheel, same verbal hook).
6. Proof with a source, or delete the number. Hidden buyers need a forwardable argument.
7. Does it feed a loop (referral, case, search, repeated CEP)?
8. Guardrails: no “cold lead,” no invented conversion, no selling unbuilt tiers, no intake-category frame.

If a checklist item fails, fix the artifact or record the exception.
